Rules

Current calculator rules and formulas.

FWCHC = 1 baseline; productivity rate = 25 lines per HC per hour.
LWC Full TimeHC = 1 baseline; productivity rate = 20 lines per HC per hour.
LWC Part TimeHC = 1 baseline; productivity rate = 15 lines per HC per hour.
TT-LineProductivity rate = 25 lines per HC per hour.
Line → HCRequired HC = target lines ÷ (hours per person × weighted rate), rounded up per block.
Remaining TimeNet work time = stop time − current time − remaining break. Estimated lines left = active HC × net hours × productivity rate.
Current Order StatusComplete Pick = max(0, Overall Order Line − MOD Balance Pick Line). Balance Pick is the manual MOD balance. For Checking Line uses the remaining difference after complete and balance.
